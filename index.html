In brief

What this work shows

BSR-DPO (Balanced Success-Recovery DPO) extends Direct Preference Optimization — a preference-based training method — to logged marketing data. Channel policies learned naively from such logs collapse to the majority channel, and weak overlap makes them hard to evaluate without circular reasoning. The three stages address this step by step:

M0

The base DPO adaptation to logged bandit data still collapses to the majority channel.

M1

Class-balanced weighting restores the preference gradient of minority-channel customers.

M2

Successful logged contacts are recovered — failed contacts are never imitated.

Screened against 13 offline baselines with SNIPS, effective-sample-size diagnostics, and reward-model-free agreement checks on the UCI Bank Marketing dataset (41,188 records) as an open proxy.
